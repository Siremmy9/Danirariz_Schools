IMAGES
======
Put school photos here. Reference them from js/config.js (images, heroPoster) or by path in Admin > Gallery / Staff / News.
Until you add photos, the site shows on-brand placeholders labelled "Replace with school photo".
Recommended: JPG or WebP, under 300 KB each. Section images portrait 4:5, news/facility images landscape 4:3 or 16:10.
Also add assets/images/og-image.jpg (1200x630) for link previews when sharing on WhatsApp/Facebook.
